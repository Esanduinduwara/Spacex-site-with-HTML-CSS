Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SpaceX, its missions, spacecraft, and technologies.

01 What is SpaceX? +

SpaceX is an aerospace company that develops rockets, spacecraft, satellite systems, and other technologies designed to advance space exploration.

02 Who founded SpaceX? +

SpaceX was founded by Elon Musk in 2002 with the goal of developing technologies that could make space exploration more accessible and eventually support human missions to Mars.

03 What is Starship? +

Starship is SpaceX's large, fully reusable launch vehicle system. It is being developed for missions involving Earth orbit and future deep-space exploration.

04 What is Starlink? +

Starlink is a satellite internet system operated by SpaceX. It uses a large constellation of satellites in low Earth orbit to provide internet connectivity.

05 What is Falcon 9? +

Falcon 9 is a two-stage orbital launch vehicle developed by SpaceX. Its first stage is designed to return to Earth and be reused.

06 Can I apply to SpaceX? +

SpaceX offers career opportunities across engineering, software, manufacturing, operations, business, and other areas. Applicants can explore available positions through the company's official careers resources.
